Our story

Made by hand. Held by heart.

Mystic Moldings is a resin art and flower preservation studio. What began with a love for pressed flowers is now demi-fine jewellery, custom décor and personalised gifts for the people you love, each one poured, set and finished by hand.

You’ll also find us at pop-ups and exhibitions with trays of rings, earrings, keychains, clocks and serving trays. Watch the Exhibitions highlight on Instagram to see where we’ll be next.
