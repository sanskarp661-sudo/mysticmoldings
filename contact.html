A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before we begin.

What can I have preserved?

Roses, wedding varmalas, bouquet flowers, and small keepsakes such as pet hair are starting points for a personalised piece. Suitability depends on the material and its condition, so discuss your idea with us before sending anything.

Can I ask about ready-made products too?

Of course. Choose “Ready-made product” in your enquiry and include the name of the jewellery, keychain, hair clip, keepsake, or other piece you’re interested in.

Will my personal story be shared?

Your story is yours to tell. We’ll ask your permission before featuring a keepsake story, and leave out personal details.
