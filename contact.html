Good to know

Before we begin.

What can I have preserved?

Proposal and date-night roses, wedding varmalas and bouquet flowers, festival blooms, and small keepsakes such as pet fur. Suitability depends on the material and its condition, so talk to us before sending anything.

Can you add names, dates or photos?

Yes. Many of our pieces are personalised with names in gold, initials, dates and photographs. Tell us what you’d like and we’ll suggest what works best for your piece.

How do I order a ready-made piece?

Tap the heart to save pieces you love, then use “Copy list & DM us” in your picks, or send us the piece names on Instagram. We’ll share prices and availability.

How should I send my flowers?

Wait for our guidance first. We’ll explain how to dry, wrap and pack your flowers so they arrive safely.

Will my personal story be shared?

Your story is yours to tell. We’ll always ask your permission before featuring a keepsake, and leave out personal details.
